🌸 New to Diwali?

Diwali in 60 seconds

Diwali (from Deepavali, "a row of lamps") is the festival of lights, celebrated by over a billion people around the world. It's about light winning over darkness, good over evil, and starting fresh with the people you love. Think Christmas and New Year's Eve rolled into one, with more sweets and a lot more sparkle.
